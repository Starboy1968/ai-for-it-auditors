## Claims to verify
Items below came from search summaries, vendor material, or common practice wording. Check before quoting.
- [ ] **COSO GenAI guidance date (Feb 2026):** the date came from a search result, not from opening the full document.
- [ ] **"5 Cs" label:** the IIA Standards (14.3, 14.4) describe criteria, condition, root cause, effect, and recommendations. "5 Cs" is common training shorthand, not an official IIA term.
- [ ] **Free-tier limits** (Gemini Notebook 100 notebooks / 50 sources; Otter Basic 300 min/month, 30-min cap, 3 lifetime imports; Claude/ChatGPT training settings): accurate as documented Oct 2026, but these change often.
- [ ] **Bridge letter "up to about 90 days":** a common practice norm from audit firm blogs, not an AICPA requirement.
- [ ] **All vendor features** (KPMG, Optro/AuditBoard, Workiva, TeamMate+, Deloitte): VENDOR CLAIMS, not independently confirmed.
- [ ] **ISACA AI Audit Toolkit** being free for members only: from a search summary.
